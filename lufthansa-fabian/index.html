What we saw at Lufthansa

lufthansagroup.com · Airline and aviation, Germany

Across the booking journey, the same fare add-ons, seat upgrades and cabin offers tend to reach every traveller in the same way, whatever their route, cabin or travel pattern.

The signal you already own is what each traveller books, changes, rebooks, cancels and never opens. That signal sits in analytics and in the booking system, not in the message the customer receives.

Cross-sell, upsell and retention campaigns are usually built by hand, per segment, per month. The traveller who is about to switch carriers this week gets the same offer as everyone else, or none at all.

How we could help Lufthansa

Three places where an AI that decides per traveller, not per segment, pays for itself in the first quarter.

Use case 1

Stop the switch before it happens

Churn signals per traveller from rebookings, cancellations and service contacts. The right offer, channel and moment, picked for each customer and tested continuously across email, SMS and airport touchpoints.

28%lower churn, measured at Flytxt telecom clients
Use case 2

Offer the next upgrade when it fits

Next best cabin upgrade, seat or add-on per traveller, triggered by how they actually fly. No more blanket upgrade mails to people who already fly in the cabin they want.

+17%category subscriptions at an OTT client
Use case 3

Keep every traveller engaged between trips

Personal content, destination and service nudges across email, SMS and airport touchpoints, so each traveller has a reason to engage with you every week, not only at booking.

+18%streaming activity at an OTT client

One smart agentic analytics loop on top of your existing Marketing Tech stack. No IT replacement, but seamless integration. First pilot live in 8-12 weeks.
