What we saw at waipu.tv

waipu.tv · Live TV streaming, Germany

Your homepage sells three plans and a Perfect Plus upgrade to every visitor the same way, whether they come for Bundesliga, kids' channels or the 4K catalogue.

Once a viewer is inside the app, what they watch, when they pause and which channels they never open is the strongest signal you have for the next upgrade and the next cancellation. Today that signal sits in analytics, not in the message they receive.

Winback, upsell and anti churn campaigns are built by hand, per segment, per month. The viewer who is about to leave this week gets the same offer as everyone else, or none at all.
